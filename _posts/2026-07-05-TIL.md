# TIL

I’m reading the book “Genetics for Dummies” by Dr. René-Fester Kratz. I’m currently on chapter 10.

In this chapter, I read that cancer is one of the potential side-effects of anabolic steroid use.

The mechanism behind this is:

* Anabolic steroids are synthetic forms of Testosterone.
* Hormones sometimes control gene expression. Testosterone, being an anabolic hormone, controls the expression of some genes.
* One such gene is a tumor-suppressing gene that produces a protein called p27. Testosterone reduces the expression of this gene.
* When the expression of this gene is reduced in muscle tissue, the cells there multiply at a faster rate.
